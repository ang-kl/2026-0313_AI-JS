Two consequences follow arithmetically rather than by opinion.

**The centre cell never moves on explode.** Its base is `(0,0,0)` and `(0,0,0)`
scaled by 1.34 is `(0,0,0)`. The other seven interior cells move outward; this one
cannot.

**The sector core sphere is unreachable in every view and control state**, because it
sits at the origin inside that cell's box and neither moves.

### Correction to the proposal's occlusion claim

The proposal states that 8 of 27 cells are "hidden" from any single viewpoint. The
geometry is right and the word is too strong for this renderer. Withheld cells draw at
0.16 opacity, so the cube is partly see-through and an occluded cell is not erased.
What is true, and is what matters for labelling: **a face behind an occupied cell at
0.9 opacity carries no legible text, and a face behind several 0.16 layers carries a
smear.** Occlusion here destroys legibility rather than presence. The proposal is
amended to say so.

## 3. Phase 1 - short codes on the cube faces

### What the text should say, which is the whole design

Position already encodes function, work layer and allocation: those are the three
axes. So a face must carry **what the geometry does not** - the count, and the
withheld state. Re-printing the axes onto the faces is decoration; printing the datum
is information.

- **Evidenced cell:** the cluster count, one or two digits.
- **Withheld cell:** a withheld mark. **Never `0`.** The cube spec requires that empty
  cells are not evidence of absence. A printed `0` asserts a measured absence, and the
  cell asserts no measurement. Printing it would breach the evidence contract in the
  most literal way available.

### How

`THREE.CanvasTexture` per distinct label, drawn once into a small offscreen canvas and
set as the material `map`. At most about a dozen distinct strings cover 27 cells, so
textures are cached by string rather than created per cell.

### Legibility budget, measured rather than hoped

The face is 0.88 world units; the camera is 38 degrees FOV at distance 5 to 15
(`:56`, `:131-132`). A face seen at 45 to 60 degrees compresses to roughly 50 to 70
per cent of its width. A one or two character glyph set at about 60 per cent of face
width survives that. A 50-character label such as `cellLabel` produces does not, which
is why it stays in the inspector where it is already correct.

### Limits that remain, and must appear in the interface rather than only here

Eight cells have no outward face at any angle and the centre cell has none at all, so
**the cube with codes still cannot show 8 of 27 counts.** The accompanying list must
carry all 27, and the interface must not imply the visible faces are the whole set.

## 4. Phase 2 - the unison circle with full labels

Per the companion proposal. Every tile faces the reader, so nothing is foreshortened,

## 5. Metric discipline

A rule the phases inherit, borrowed from outside this repository and carrying its
source, because a practice adopted without its origin is a preference.

### The rule

- **(a) Nothing is displayed that is not computed from the model.** No constant stands
  in for a reading.
- **(b) A placeholder reads empty, never plausible.** Before the first measurement a
  figure shows a dash or a zero, not a representative value.
- **(c) A derived or estimated figure is labelled as one, in the interface** - not only
  in a code comment where no reader will meet it.
- **(d) A count carries the scope that makes it readable:** its denominator, and where
  withholding exists, the withheld remainder.

### Where it came from, and the pair that makes the case

Two single-file repositories by one author, published weeks apart, doing opposite
things with the same idea.

`anyel1to/atsmatrix-nexus` computes every figure on its HUD each frame from live
state, and ships them initialised to `-` and `0` rather than to a flattering number.
`anyel1to/atsmatrix-agent-visualizeR--ANYEL1TO` ships `2,450 MSG/S` and `0.8ms
LATENCY` as literal strings in its markup, with no throughput or frame-rate
calculation anywhere in the file, and - with no API key configured - prints
*"Consensus reached with 98.4% confidence rating"* as agent output over a verification
that never ran. The first is the model for this rule; the second is the failure it
prevents.

And the better repository supplies part (c) by falling short of it: its cluster figure
is `agents.length / (8 + links.length / 30)`, which its own code comment calls a
*"rough connected components proxy"* while the interface labels it simply "Clusters".
**The code was honest and the label was not** - which is the version of this defect
that survives review, because nothing in the source is false.

### Audit of the current Business Cube HUD against the rule

Measured at `eaa38da`. `BusinessRubiksCube.jsx:288-291` renders three figures, all
from `businessCubeModel.js`:

| Figure | Source | (a) computed | (c) honestly labelled | (d) scoped |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `evidencedCells` | `cells.filter((cell) => cell.count > 0).length` | Pass | Pass, a true count | **Fail** |
| `clusters` | `clusters.length` | Pass | Pass | n/a |
| `agents` | `agents.length` | Pass | Pass | n/a |

**Parts (a) and (b) pass outright, and that is a result rather than an absence of
findings.** All three totals are real counts over real collections. There is no
constant on the cube's HUD and no estimate presented as a measurement. The defect the
rule exists to catch is not present here.

**Part (d) fails on one figure, and it matters more here than it would elsewhere.**
The header renders `{evidencedCells} evidenced cells` with no denominator and no
withheld count. A reader meets "7 evidenced cells" and finishes the sentence
themselves: *so the other twenty are empty*. That is the exact inference the cube spec
forbids, because the rest are **withheld**, and withholding is not evidence of
absence. **The number is true and the sentence it sits in misleads** - which is harder
to catch than a false number, and is the same shape as a "Clusters" label over a
proxy.

**Remedy, owed by whichever phase lands first:** `7 of 27 cells evidenced, 20
withheld`. The same figure, its scope restored, and the withheld count saying what the
silence is. One line of text, no model change.

### Applied to Phase 1

The face codes fall under all four parts. (a) the digit comes from `cell.count`;
(b) a cell whose model has not resolved shows nothing, never a placeholder digit;
(c) a count is a count and needs no estimate label; (d) cannot be carried by the faces,
because eight of them are never visible - so the accompanying list carries it, showing
all 27 with their scope. The withheld mark already required by section 3 is part (d)
at the level of a single cell.

### Applied to Phase 2

The same, and one addition: any aggregate the circle introduces - a ring total, a
function-arc total - is new surface, and each states its denominator **at the point of
display** rather than in a legend a reader may not reach.

### The standing prohibition, and how it is enforced

No figure on either surface may be rounded up, smoothed, estimated or interpolated to
look more complete than the evidence. Where a figure cannot be computed it is withheld
and said to be withheld.

**This is enforced by assertion, not by memory.** Every metric these phases add carries
a browser assertion that the rendered text equals the model's computed value for the
same fixture, so a constant cannot be substituted later without turning a test red. A
rule that lives only in this document is a rule that survives exactly as long as the
person who read it.

## 6. Gates

- **CLAUDE.md section 4** fires on all three phases: `conformance-auditor` and
  `a11y-honesty-reviewer` before merge, no colour as the sole signal, 44px targets,
  aria labels, and the `AI-assisted; human decides` footer with the Source /
  Confidence / Time-window line.
- **R007**, no non-ASCII in JSX string literals. Canvas text is not a JSX literal, but
  the withheld mark must render in the canvas font on every platform, so prefer ASCII.
  The house pattern where a non-ASCII glyph is genuinely needed is
  `String.fromCharCode(0x00b7)`, as at `ReviewStudioLegacy.jsx:24`.
- **Rules V-1 and V-2.** Phases 1 and 2 are `new_feature_added`, a minor bump needing
  Human Lead confirmation. Phase 0 is `bug_fix`, a patch, confirmation optional.

## 7. Sequencing

Phase 0 is independent and can land alone.

If both labelling phases are wanted, run **0, then 2, then 1**. Phase 2 subsumes
Phase 1's purpose, so once the circle carries the full labels the cube's codes become
a convenience rather than the only route to the numbers, and their design gets simpler
and less pressured. Doing 1 first would tune the cube's codes to carry a burden that
Phase 2 then removes.

## 8. Decisions for the Human Lead

1. Phase 0 option: (a), (b) or (c). Recommended (c), remove the sphere.
2. Phase 1, Phase 2, or both.
3. The withheld mark. ASCII preferred; it must not read as a quantity.
4. The version bump, per Rule V-1.
5. **The `evidencedCells` scope fix** from section 5: `7 of 27 cells evidenced, 20
   withheld` in place of `7 evidenced cells`. Recommended in **Phase 0**, since it is
   one line of text, changes no model, and is `bug_fix` shaped rather than a feature.
