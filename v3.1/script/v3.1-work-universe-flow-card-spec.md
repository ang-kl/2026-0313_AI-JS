# v3.1 Work Universe Flow-Card Geometry

**Reference:** user-supplied Pinch AI workflow screenshot, 27-08-2026.

## Presentation contract

The Level-1 Work Universe uses the reference's visual grammar without copying its product semantics:

- a dotted working canvas;
- rounded rectangular cards rather than circular islands;
- one stable evidence anchor;
- blue, rounded branch connectors;
- clear active and dimmed states;
- compact card content that keeps all three signals interactive.

The five canonical graphs remain parallel projections of the same anchor. Connector routing must not imply that one canonical graph happens before another.

## Responsive contract

- Desktop and wide tablet: the anchor occupies the left column; three graph cards occupy the middle column and two occupy the right column.
- Phone: the anchor and five graph cards form one vertical card stack. A separate left-side branch spine connects the anchor directly to each graph card; it is not a sequential flow.
- Existing `ResizeObserver` measurements remain authoritative for card width and text scaling.
- The source rail, Contents tree, detail inspector, dedicated maps, and visual selector keep their existing independent scrolling behavior.

## Non-regression contract

The shape change does not alter:

- the five graph definitions or 15 first-order signals;
- payload, projection, withholding, or evidence-link algorithms;
- Role Graph/FAB integration;
- Organisation, Workflow, Value Stream, or AI Moments routes;
- Print/PDF output;
- Step 1 or Step 2;
- `v3/` or the Railway root.
