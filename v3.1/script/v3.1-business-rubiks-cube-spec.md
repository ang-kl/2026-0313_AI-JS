# v3.1 Business Rubik's Cube

## Purpose

The Company AI Moments `Business cube` view replaces the former one-function-to-many-duties Neural diagram. It is a movable, sector-centred allocation view over the existing deterministic company-duty model. It does not alter duty extraction, clustering, recurrence, exposure, promotion, SAT, or provenance.

## Evidence contract

- The centre names the organisation's exact ACRA primary SSIC description and code.
- Without an exact ACRA match and primary SSIC description, the centre reads `Sector evidence withheld`.
- Step 2's SSOC occupational family is never relabelled as an industry sector.
- Empty cells remain visible as withheld cells. They are not evidence of absence.
- Every selected duty retains its posting recurrence and provenance-row count.

## Axes

The cube is a stable 3 x 3 x 3 evidence matrix:

1. `Function`: up to three slots from supplied MCF categories. More than three are represented by the two most evidenced functions plus `Other supplied functions`; missing slots are withheld.
2. `Work layer`: `Execute` for Activity/MEDIUM, `Coordinate` for Coordination/LOW, and `Govern` for Accountability/Relational/Judgment/HUMAN.
3. `Allocation`: `Human-held`, `Augmentation review`, and `Agent candidate`, using the existing exposure and promotion result.

This is an organisation work-allocation lens, not an organisation chart, value stream, process model, workforce forecast, or deployment recommendation.

## Interaction contract

- Pointer/touch: orbit, pan, zoom, click a cubelet, and use front/right/top/reset/explode controls.
- Keyboard: arrow keys rotate, `+`/`-` zoom, and `0` resets while the cube has focus.
- The evidence matrix provides the same cell selection without requiring 3D interaction.
- No auto-rotation. Reduced-motion preferences disable damped movement.
- Mobile stacks the inspector below the cube; desktop docks it beside the cube.

## Reference frame

The dimensions follow established business-architecture practice without claiming standards conformance: OMG Business Architecture's capability/value/organisation relationship view, TOGAF business capability and organisation mapping, and sector taxonomies such as APQC PCF and BIAN. Current source evidence supports functions, work layers, allocation, and ACRA SSIC sector only. Value streams, organisation units, BIAN service domains, and sector process frameworks remain future evidence integrations.

## Non-regression boundary

Cards remain available. Shared Role Graph and WikiGraph renderers are unchanged. Step 2 behaviour, `v3/`, Railway configuration, Workflow Map, Value Stream Map, Organisation Map evidence rules, and the AI Moments analysis model are unchanged.
