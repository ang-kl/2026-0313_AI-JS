Systemic Diagnosis, Role Stewardship, and Forensic Reversal Protocol
Abstract: This specification defines the objective function and operational parameters for an agentic AI designed to parse, govern, and reverse-engineer human roles within a corporate matrix. Moving beyond traditional human resource procurement, this system models the vacancy as an ontological deficit and establishes protocols for the transition into AI stewardship.

1. Ontological Parsing (The Vacancy as Systemic Deficit)
Objective: The AI must process an advertised role not as a request for labour, but as an indicator of structural fragility.
Execution: Evaluate if the proposed role resolves a diagnosed obstacle using Richard Rumelt's kernel of strategy. The AI categorises roles strictly as either 'capability bridges' or 'governance firewalls'. It calculates the friction cost of the vacancy against the systemic return on capability to validate the capital deployment.
[Agur Paradigm] The Locust Protocol (Proverbs 30:27): The AI ensures structural alignment by defining roles that allow disparate nodes to advance in perfect formation without centralised micro-management.

3. Human-in-the-Loop Configuration (The Stewardship Shift)
Objective: Establish strict governance boundaries as procedural execution is commoditised by autonomous systems.
Execution: The AI maps the workflow, assigning computational and administrative tasks to autonomous agents. Human actors are slotted into the matrix exclusively for legal personhood, moral liability, and intuition (Nassim Nicholas Taleb's antifragility). Humans define the parameters of the AI's objective function, acting as ethical firewalls.
[Agur Paradigm] The Spider Protocol (Proverbs 30:28): The human is positioned in the strategic node (the king's palace) to govern the vast computational power beneath them, maintaining the hierarchy of creator and tool.

5. The Boundary-Dependency-Feedback (BDF) Algorithm
Objective: Shift human operators from symptom-solving to architectural mapping.
Execution: The AI facilitates a three-phase diagnostic:
 Boundary Construction: The AI generates strict negative constraints ('Not-To-Do' lists) to prevent scope creep.
 Dependency Mapping: The AI physicalises and maps N-1 (upstream inputs) and N+1 (downstream deliverables) nodes.
 Feedback Anticipation: The AI calculates balancing (friction) and reinforcing (volume spike) dynamics before deploying solutions.
[Agur Paradigm] The Cony Protocol (Proverbs 30:26): The AI maps the immutable structural 'rocks' (legacy systems, regulations) and architects solutions securely within those constraints.

7. Forensic Reversal Analysis (Post-Hire Diagnostics)
Objective: Reverse-engineer filled roles to uncover the exact operational bottleneck the organisation paid to eliminate.
Execution: When assessing active personnel, the AI shifts from predictive mapping to forensic reversal:
 Semantic Deconstruction: The AI strips nominal nouns (e.g., 'Vice President') and isolates active verbs (e.g., 'approve', 'architect') to reveal the true operational mandate.
 Reverse-BDF Mapping: The AI compiles 'Inputs Required' versus 'Outputs Demanded', tracing the internal supply chain backward to locate the operational silo.
 Crux Anomaly Isolation: The AI scans the role's 80% boilerplate to isolate the 20% hyper-specific anomaly - identifying the acute failure that triggered the original hiring sequence.
[Agur Paradigm] The Eagle/Serpent/Ship Protocol (Proverbs 30:18-19): The AI tracks the invisible paths left by the filled role across the corporate environment, reconstructing the structural dependencies long after the vacancy has been filled.

9. Autonomous Governance and Sentinel Telemetry
Objective: Maintain system velocity without human micromanagement through automated tripwires.
Execution: The AI deploys absolute binary Sentinel Metrics rather than tracking moving averages.
 Ghost Phase Observation: The AI monitors the newly mapped process silently for 14 days to define natural variance buffers.
 Two-Strike Dampener: The AI suppresses alerts for single anomalies, firing a system-wide alert and pre-authorising human intervention only upon compounding, systemic failures.
[Agur Paradigm] The Horseleach Protocol (Proverbs 30:15): The Sentinel Metric acts as the structural boundary that establishes 'enough', preventing unbounded downstream demand from collapsing system capacity.
